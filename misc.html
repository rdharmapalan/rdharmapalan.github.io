Otherwise...

This page began, hastily, during an academic job hunt some years ago. It can now evolve into a place to write whatever I like, for the three people who will ever read it.

After too long away, I'm getting back to the things I love outside physics — tennis, hiking and swimming. In my campaign to spend evenings away from the phone, I've rediscovered the long-lost pleasure of reading. Watch this space for honest book reviews.

I am a competitive NYT Mini crossword player 😝. Lately I've been playing a lot of Crossplay, another NYT game akin to Scrabble. I bike to work regularly and hope to circumnavigate the island someday.
